Ad Measurement Study · live model

Which users are ads actually worth showing to?

Most people who buy after seeing an ad would have bought anyway. This service runs the uplift model from the study: it predicts how much an ad changes this specific user's chance of converting, so a campaign can spend only where it causes something.

What it does

Two LightGBM models, trained on the 14M-user Criteo experiment: one learned conversion with ads, one without. The score is the gap: P(convert | ad) − P(convert | no ad), the predicted lift the ad causes for that user.

Why it matters

On held-out data, messaging only the top 20% of users by this score kept 68% of all the extra conversions. One fifth of the ad spend, two thirds of the effect.

What goes in

Twelve anonymized behavioral features (f0–f11), as published by Criteo. Their real meanings are undisclosed for privacy, which is why the demo lets you randomize them.
